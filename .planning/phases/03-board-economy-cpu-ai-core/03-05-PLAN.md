1. **`board/victory.ts` `endGame(state, rules, events): GameState`** (signature unchanged; randomness comes from `nextInt(state.hidden.rng, ...)` directly and the advanced rng is written back, because both callers (`turn/end`, `week/advance`) are fixed by earlier plans). Eligible = the award has a counter and the maximum over players is `> 0`. Draw `min(3, eligible.length)` distinct awards by a partial Fisher-Yates over the eligible list (for `i` in `0..k-1`: `j = nextInt(i, n - 1)`, swap `i` and `j`; the first `k` are the awards, kept in pool order for the event). Winner of an award: the player(s) with the maximum counter; ties go to the LOWEST assets (assets before any bonus of this call), then the lowest seat. Each award adds `rules.board.awardBonus` to the winner's `bonus`. `Award` = `{id, playerId, bonus: awardBonus}`.
   - **Tally** (after awards): for each player `{gold, items, towns, bonus, assets}` where `gold` is the character gold, `towns` the sum of owned town values, `bonus` the final `bonus[p]`, `assets = assetsOf(state, rules, p)` (D17, includes `bonus`) and `items = assets - gold - towns - bonus` (items, scrolls and equipped gear at `sellBp`; assert this identity in tests).
